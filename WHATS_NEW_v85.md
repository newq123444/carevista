# CareVista v85 — simple view is for carers and senior carers

- **Simple / Full view toggle** now appears only for **carers and senior carers**, and is on
  by default for both. Previously anyone could switch it on — kitchen, housekeeping and
  maintenance staff got a carer's round of care tasks that weren't theirs.
- Anyone else who had switched it on before is put back on the full view automatically.
- **Senior carers' simple menu now includes Medications**, because many give medication.
- **Managers, deputies and nurses get "Preview carer view"** in the menu: the carer's home
  screen inside their normal app, clearly labelled. Useful for checking, training and
  showing to a manager. It uses real tasks — anything recorded from it is in your name.

No migration in this release. v84's migrations still apply if you haven't run them.
